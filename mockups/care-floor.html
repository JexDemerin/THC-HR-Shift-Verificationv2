The care floor

The Together Homecare office team, each as their own dog. When someone updates the CRM, their dog gets to work at their desk and the update shows up in a bubble and in the activity log. Between updates the team heads to meetings, the printer and the break room. The people and breeds are real; the updates in this mockup are made up so you can see how it would look.

    Updates come in

    In the real build, a small Vercel function reads the CRM's change history every few seconds, the same way the post office follows n8n.

    Each person has a dog

    Who made the change decides which dog moves. Breeds and desks are fixed per person, so everyone's dog looks the same every day.

    Watch only

    The floor only reads updates. It never changes the CRM, and only people signed in to your Vercel team can open it.
