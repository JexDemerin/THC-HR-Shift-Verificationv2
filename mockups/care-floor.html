The care floor

The Together Homecare office team, each as their own dog. Each cubicle is decorated for where its owner is from: Bataan, Cavite, Quezon Province and Pampanga in the Philippines, and a different California landmark for everyone in California. When someone updates the CRM, their dog gets to work in their cubicle and the update shows up in a bubble and in the activity log. Each dog also follows Time Doctor: it walks in through the door when its owner starts tracking and leaves when they stop. A paid break sends the dog to the sofa, the cafeteria or the lounge; lunch is an unpaid break in the cafeteria. The people and breeds are real; the updates in this mockup are made up so you can see how it would look.

    Updates come in

    In the real build, a small Vercel function reads the CRM's change history every few seconds, the same way the post office follows n8n.

    Each person has a dog

    Who made the change decides which dog moves. Breeds and desks are fixed per person, so everyone's dog looks the same every day.

    Watch only

    The floor only reads updates. It never changes the CRM, and only people signed in to your Vercel team can open it.
