The care floor

Every caregiver on the roster gets a dog avatar, and the breed is picked from their name. As WellSky shift updates come in, the dogs act them out: they head to the client's home, clock in at the door, post care log notes while they work, then clock out and send their care log to the HR desk, where Rosa verifies the hours into the Payroll tab. Between shifts they nap in the lounge. This mockup plays a made-up day so you can see how it would look.

    Shift updates come in

    In the real build, a small Vercel function reads the latest rows of the Shift Log sheet that the extension fills from WellSky, every few seconds, the same way the post office follows n8n.

    Staff become dogs

    Each caregiver name maps to a breed. The breed sits in a column next to their name, so it stays the same every day and HR can change it.

    Watch only

    The floor only reads shift updates. It never edits WellSky or the sheet, and it is limited to people signed in to your Vercel team.
